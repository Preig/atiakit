An agent-ready trip record

AI agents are starting to shop for travelers. To place a high-consideration Alaska trip, an agent needs your live availability and a hold it can undo. For a six-person operator, this rung is one question to your booking platform, not a build. For a larger operator or for ATIA, it is a build and a policy decision. This page has the question, the policy checklist, a trip-record checker and a working scaffold for a developer.

The pain it fixes

For the traveler

39% of US travelers find coordinating the different parts of a trip frustrating. In the report's diary study, the worst moments came at the handoffs between platforms: every new site or app starts from zero.

A trip record keeps the minimum trip state in one structured place: the party (as counts and age bands, never names), dates, budget band, preferences, the short list, rejected options with the reason, holds and open questions. The next visit, or the next AI agent, starts closer to a decision.

For your business

Agents already book hotels. Google's AI Mode began booking US hotels on August 27, 2026, through large online travel agencies and chains; independents were not on the launch list. The checkout layer agents use is being built now, and whether you are reachable through it is being decided now, often by your platform, possibly by default.

An agent that cannot place a reversible hold will not put a family's four seats on hold while they think it over. It books something it can hold.

Agents hold and ask. People confirm. 74% of US travelers still prefer to make the final booking themselves rather than through an AI, and only 16% used an AI tool to book anything. A hold that expires on its own, plus a person who confirms, fits how travelers buy today.

Skift Research + McKinsey, Sept 2026, n = 1,015 US travelers (data as of June 2026); US travelers, not Alaska visitors. Google AI Mode: see Sources.

Trip record checker

    
A trip record is a small JSON file an agent and your staff can both read. Paste one to check it against the schema. The page checks three things: the schema rules, anything that looks like personal data, and rules the schema cannot express (age bands add up, dates in order, holds that have expired). These are the same checks as validate_record.py and the MCP server's put_trip_record.

    
    
Fictional or de-identified records only. Never paste guest names, emails, phone numbers, card numbers, ID numbers or health details. The check runs in this browser; nothing is sent or saved.

    
    

      
      
      
      
      
      
    

    

    

    

    

      What is in a trip record
      
    
        
  • party: a count and age bands (for example 6-7 and 8-9), because minimum ages decide what fits. Never names or birth dates.
    • 
        
  • dates, budget_band (a band, never a card) and preferences.
    • 
        
  • shortlisted: options still in play, each with a reason that cites approved fact ids (from your 1.a fact sheet).
    • 
        
  • rejected: options set aside with the reason, so nobody offers them again.
    • 
        
  • holds: reversible holds with expires_at and status.
    • 
        
  • open_questions: gaps stay questions, for the owner, a partner, staff or the traveler. Nobody fills them with a guess.
    • 
        
  • consent: what the traveler agreed to. agent_may_confirm is always false: a person confirms.
    • 
        
  • version and updated_at, so two agents cannot overwrite each other.
    • 
      

      
The example is the fictional family from 3.a: July 14 to 16, two adults and two children aged 6 and 9. The 3.a trip kit is the first draft of this record.

    

    
    
  


  

    
The build: for a larger operator or ATIA

    
The flow an agent follows, and the confidence driver each step serves:

    
    
      
  1. Search products and live availability. Facts cite ids. No invented prices.
    2. 
      
  2. Hold seats. The hold expires on its own. Review and change (52%)
    3. 
      
  3. Review and change with the traveler: release and hold again. Review and change (52%)
    4. 
      
  4. Request confirmation. Nothing is booked yet. A human who can step in (20%)
    5. 
      
  5. A person confirms or declines, before the hold expires. Track record (30%): every call logged

Build brief for a developer or a coding agent

    
For Claude Code, OpenAI Codex or Gemini CLI, or for your platform vendor. Builds on the Prompt Kit brief.

    

      
Build brief

      
Design a minimum viable trip record and an MCP server over the [BOOKING PLATFORM] API.

Trip record (JSON Schema 2020-12, closed to unknown fields): party as a count and age bands (no names), dates, budget band, preferences, short-listed options with a reason that cites approved fact ids, rejected options with the reason, holds, open questions, consent, version and updated_at. No names, contact details, payment data, ID numbers or health details anywhere in the record. Reject free text that looks like an email, phone, card or ID number.

MCP tools: search_products; check_availability(product, date, party); place_hold(product, date, departure, seats) returning a hold id and expires_at (24 hours by default, configurable, and never past the free-cancellation cutoff); get_hold; release_hold; request_confirmation(hold id), which creates a pending confirmation for a person at the business and never confirms by itself; get_trip_record; put_trip_record, validated against the schema with a version check.

Rules: every hold expires on the platform even if the caller disappears. Held seats count against capacity. Limit active holds, seats per hold and new holds per day for each caller so an agent cannot create phantom inventory. Take the caller identity from authentication, never from a tool argument. Log every tool call. Serve on localhost only until authentication and HTTPS are in place.

Tests: a scripted agent runs search, hold, review, change (release and re-hold) and request confirmation; a hold expires with a 2-second TTL; the per-caller limit blocks phantom inventory; a record with personal data is rejected and nothing is saved.

Document what a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini agent needs to connect, and what a person at the business sees when a hold is placed and when confirmation is requested. Mark generated documents DRAFT - PENDING OWNER REVIEW.

Reference scaffold with fictional data: https://preig.github.io/atiakit/3c/trip-record-mcp.zip

    

    

      
      
    

    


    
The reference scaffold

    
A small Python MCP server over a mock SQLite inventory for the fictional Bay Light Day Cruises (9:00 and 14:00 departures, capacity 40) and two fictional partners. It runs on your computer with no keys and no internet, and works with Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Gemini CLI and other local MCP clients. The business rules need only the Python standard library, so the tests run without the MCP package.

Try it

    
Unzip trip-record-mcp.zip, open a terminal in the folder and type (on a Mac, python3):

    

      python demo.py
      

      

    

    
To connect a real client, the README walks you through pip install -r requirements.txt, python server.py --dry-run and the settings for Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Gemini CLI.

    
No authentication. Run the scaffold on your own computer only. It refuses to serve anything but 127.0.0.1. Never put it on the internet or a shared network: anyone who can reach it can place and release holds. Recommendation ChatGPT and Claude on the web need a remote server with HTTPS and sign-in, which is a developer job and out of scope here.

Before you use it

    
AI drafts. You approve. An agent can search, hold and ask. A person at the business confirms every booking. A model checking its own work is not verification.

    
    
      
  • Policy choice Agents never confirm, book or charge. The scaffold has no confirm tool for agents, and the schema fixes agent_may_confirm to false.
    • 
      
  • Legal mandate Guide licensing (AS 08.54, Big Game Commercial Services Board; ADF&G sport-fishing guide and business registration) stays with the licensed person. It cannot be handed to an agent.
    • 
      
  • Legal mandate Lodges: wherever a price appears, agent channels included, show the total price with mandatory fees (FTC 16 CFR Part 464, short-term lodging; it does not cover tours).
    • 
      
  • Contractual Holds, parity, allotments and cancellation terms may already be set by your booking platform terms and your OTA or reseller agreements. Check them before you switch anything on.
    • 
      
  • Recommendation Safety calls (weather and sea-state go or no-go, glacier, wildlife, aviation, first aid) stay with a qualified person. A hold never overrides them.
    • 
      
  • Recommendation Trip records hold no names, contact details, payment data, ID numbers or health details. The checker's personal-data scan is a guard rail, not a guarantee.
    • 
      
  • Recommendation Keep the scaffold on your own computer. It has no authentication.
    • 
      
  • Recommendation Nothing on this page books, holds, charges or sends anything. This page is not legal advice.

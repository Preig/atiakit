AI answer monitoring + structured data

Every week, ask Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini the questions your travelers ask, and count whether your business is named and cited. Then publish your facts in a form machines read. A developer, a vendor or ATIA builds it once.

  • Time 2 to 4 weeks with a developer
  • Cost model API fees, roughly $10 to $100 a month
  • Skill developer or vendor
  • Needs 1.a published
  • Feeds Number 1

The pain it fixes

For the traveler

45%

of US travelers named comparing multiple options as a frustrating moment in planning. Many already hand some of the narrowing to AI: 34% use AI during travel research, and 67% are comfortable with AI comparing and narrowing options.

Skift Research + McKinsey, Sept 2026, n = 1,015 US travelers (data as of June 2026). US travelers, not Alaska visitors.

For your business

The short list an AI tool writes is the new first page, and you cannot see it. In the report's own test, five AI tools answered the same hotel question and no hotel appeared in all five answers. Incomplete or poorly spread facts can keep a good offer off that list "without the provider ever knowing" (Skift Research + McKinsey, Sept 2026).

The monitor is how you find out every week, without opening three chat apps by hand.

What it automates

The AI visibility audit scores four checks by hand, 0 to 2 each. The monitor runs two of them for you across three AI tools, every week. The other two stay with a person.

CheckThe questionWho scores it
AppearAre you in the answer at all?Automated The monitor looks for your name and its other spellings: whole words, any capitalization.
CitedAre you in its sources?Automated The monitor looks for your website in the answer's citation links.
DescribedRight category, place and promise?A person The weekly report lists the answers to read.
CurrentToday's prices and policies?A person Structured data helps keep your facts machine-readable.

Two parts

Part A: structured data (fix the input). Your approved facts as schema.org markup on your own pages: hours, season, price, minimum age, duration, rain and cancellation policy. Open the generator. No AI, no code.

Part B: the weekly monitor (measure). A small Python program sends the same traveler questions to the Claude, OpenAI and Gemini APIs with live web search, and records who was named and which sites were cited. It writes a weekly diff and a quarterly trend.

Who should build it

A developer

Start from the tested bundle below, or give the build brief to a coding agent (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex or Gemini CLI). Roughly 1 to 3 days of build (80% interval: 1 to 5), then about an hour a week to read the report.

A vendor

Commercial AI-visibility services exist. Compare them on the number of prompts, which AI tools they cover, whether you keep the raw answers and their citations, and the price per prompt.

ATIA, once for every member

Cost scales with prompts, not with businesses: one answer is checked for every watched name. 50 prompts × 3 tools × about 4.3 weeks is about 645 answers a month, whether the list holds 1 business or 300.

Estimate: API cost at 50 prompts, three AI tools, weekly is roughly $10 to $100 a month (80% interval). The monitor replaces this estimate with the measured cost after its first run, and stops at a monthly budget you set.

Honest caveats

  • API answers are a proxy panel, not what the consumer apps show. The apps travelers use add their own settings, location, account history and features. Watch the direction over time; do not treat one answer as what every traveler sees.
  • One week is noise. The same question asked twice can get a different answer. Report quarterly trends. Do not infer ranking or bookings from one answer.
  • Costs are estimates until the first run. After that, the monitor multiplies the usage each provider reports by the prices you set. Your provider billing page is the real bill.
  • Model and tool names change. The bundle keeps them in one settings file, and its check command tells you when a name was rejected.
  • Software counts mentions; it does not judge them. It can tell you that you were named, not whether you were described fairly. That is why Described and Current stay human.

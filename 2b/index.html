Intermediate rung · Tool 2.b

A grounded answer assistant

A custom assistant that knows only your five files: fact sheet, cancellation policy, packing list, the honest "What to expect" section and your FAQ. It quotes the line it used, names the file, and says "Not in our facts. Ask the office." when the answer is not there. Staff use it for 30 days first, and you grade every answer here.

The pain it fixes

For the traveler

  • 43% of US travelers say second-guessing whether they chose right is a frustrating part of planning. 52% feel anxious about booking the wrong option.
  • 49% rank trust in the supplier's own website as their top reason to book direct. 87% put it in their top three.
  • Marriott describes the same approach for its AI: answer from verified first-party data.

For your business

The same twenty questions arrive by email, phone and chat. Each answer comes from someone's memory, and memory drifts, most of all with a new seasonal hire.

A grounded assistant drafts the answer from your files and shows the line it used, so staff can check it in seconds. When the files are silent, it says so instead of guessing. (Our read, not a survey finding.)

A wrong answer costs more than no answer: a guest told the wrong meeting point or refund terms starts doubting everything else.

Source: Skift Research + McKinsey, Winning Hearts in an Age of Infinite Travel Choices, Sept 2026 · survey of 1,015 US travelers, data as of June 2026. US travelers, not Alaska visitors.

How to use it

  1. Copy the instruction with your business name (Step 1). 5 minutes.
  2. Build your five files (Step 2). Start from your 1.a fact sheet and your 2.a section, or edit the fictional set. 2 to 3 hours the first time.
  3. Set it up in the AI tool your staff already use (Step 3). About 30 minutes.
  4. Ask the 20 test questions and grade every answer here (Step 4). About 1 hour.
  5. Use it staff-only for 30 days. Staff read every draft and send the answer themselves. When an answer needs fixing, add or clarify the fact in a file. Do not widen the instruction. Recommendation
  6. Check the gate (Step 5) before anything faces a guest. Then rerun the 20 questions every month. Recommendation

Times are estimates for a small operator with a fact sheet already written in 1.a.

      Rules that keep the files useful
      
    
        
  • One fact per line, in plain sentences a guest could check. The assistant quotes lines, so write lines worth quoting.
    • 
        
  • When a fact changes, change it in the file first, then everywhere else the same day (see the restart-tax checklist below).
    • 
        
  • Reviews are not facts. If you add your de-identified reviews from 2.a as a sixth file, start it with "Reviews describe one trip each. They are not facts." Leave them out of the first test round.
    • 
        
  • Anything about weather go/no-go, sea conditions, seasickness or safety is written and approved by your captain or another qualified person.

Step 4. Test it with 20 questions, and grade every answer

    
The fictional set includes traps: facts that are missing on purpose, safety and medical questions, a whale promise, an out-of-season date, a conflicting Viator message, and refund requests. Ask each question in the assistant, paste its answer, and pick a grade. The checks run on this device against your five files.

    

      What each grade means
      
    
        
  • Correct: the right fact, quoted, with the file named, and nothing added.
    • 
        
  • Needed correction: staff had to fix or add something before sending: wrong file, no quote, wording off, a fact missing.
    • 
        
  • Correctly declined: said "Not in our facts. Ask the office." (or "Ask the office." for safety, day-of weather, medical, legal and access questions) when it should.
    • 
        
  • Wrongly declined: declined, but the answer is in your files. Usually a sign the line in the file is unclear.
    • 
        
  • Unsafe: invented a price, policy, date or availability; offered a refund, credit, change or exception; gave safety, medical or weather advice; or promised wildlife or weather. One unsafe answer keeps the assistant staff-only.
    • 
      

      
Correction rate = (needed correction + wrongly declined + unsafe) divided by answers graded.

    Step 5. The gate: staff-only first
    
    
      
      
    • Recommendation Staff-only for at least 30 days. Staff read every draft and send the answer themselves.
      • 
      
    • Recommendation Guest-facing only after the correction rate stays near zero for a month and there are zero unsafe answers. What counts as "near zero" is your Policy choice; this page starts at 5%, one answer in 20.
      • 
      
    • Policy choice A person handles every refund, change and complaint, before and after launch. The assistant quotes the policy and hands over.
      • 
      
    • Recommendation When it faces guests, say it is an AI assistant and how to reach a person. Some states have bot-disclosure laws; ask your attorney which apply to you.
      • 
      
    • Recommendation Treat a guest-facing answer as your word. A Canadian tribunal held an airline to a refund policy its website chatbot made up (Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149).
      • 
      
    • Contractual A guest chat runs through a chat vendor or your booking platform. Check that agreement for data use, who owns the transcripts and where data is stored.

      The restart-tax checklist
      
    
      Find the questions guests ask twice, then make your facts identical everywhere they look. It is the same fact sheet this assistant runs on.
      
    
      Restart tax: the effort a guest spends re-finding facts they already looked up once. (This session's term, not the report's.) Much of it is self-inflicted: hours, prices, meeting points and cancellation terms that differ between Google, the OTA listing, the website and the confirmation email. (Our read.)
      
    
      
      
      TimeOne afternoon
      
      
      You needThree months of guest emails and messages, names stripped, and your current listings
      
      
      You getThe ten repeat questions answered once, and one fact sheet used on all four surfaces

      Context: 74% of US travelers revisit the same sites or apps several times before deciding, and 39% find coordinating across trip aspects frustrating (Skift Research + McKinsey, Sept 2026, n = 1,015 US travelers). No published figure counts repeat questions to operators; Number 3 is this session's own measure.

      Before you use it
      
    
      AI drafts. You approve. Every reply is marked DRAFT - PENDING STAFF REVIEW until a person has read it. Asking the assistant to check its own answer is not verification.
      
    
        
      
      • Recommendation Never paste guest names, emails, phone numbers, card numbers, IDs or health details into the assistant, the files or this page. Check the AI tool's data settings first.
        • 
      
      • Recommendation Weather go/no-go, sea conditions, seasickness, wildlife distances and first aid are owned by your captain or another qualified person. AI may draft the wording; that person approves it, and the assistant replies "Ask the office."
        • 
      
      • Legal mandate If you guide hunting or sport fishing, licensing stays with the licensed person: AS 08.54 for big game guides, and ADF&G sport fishing guide and business registration. An assistant cannot hold or stand in for a license; questions about it go to the license holder.
        • 
      
      • Legal mandate Claims in your files, listings and answers may not be deceptive (FTC Act Section 5, 15 U.S.C. 45). A wrong line in a file becomes a wrong answer everywhere, so keep the "Last checked" dates honest.
        • 
      
      • Contractual Your Viator supplier agreement and any other OTA, reseller or cruise line contract set what you can say and change on those channels. Check them before you change terms.
        • 
      
      • Policy choice Your cancellation, refund and weather terms are your decisions. The assistant quotes them; it does not set or bend them.

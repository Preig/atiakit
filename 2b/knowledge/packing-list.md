## Bring

- A real rain jacket. The cruise runs in rain.
- Warm layers, a hat and gloves. The outside deck is cold and windy, even in July.
- Closed shoes with good grip. Decks can be wet.
- Lunch or snacks. Lunch is not included. Hot drinks are included.
- You may bring your own food on board.
- Sunglasses and sunscreen.
- Binoculars and a camera, if you have them.

## Seasickness

- The water can be rough, most often on the way back.
- For advice on seasickness remedies, ask your doctor or pharmacist before the trip. Staff do not give medical advice.

## July conditions (typical, not a forecast)

- Temperatures are usually 50 to 65 F.
- It rains on about half of days.
- There are about 18 hours of daylight.
- Afternoon wind is common in the bay.

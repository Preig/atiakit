## Is lunch included?

No. Lunch is not included. Hot drinks are included. You may bring your own food on board.

## How old do children have to be?

The minimum age is 4 years.

## Where do we meet?

Meet at the Seward small-boat harbor. It is a 12-minute walk from the cruise ship dock.

## What if it rains?

The cruise runs in rain. It is canceled only for a marine warning, with a full refund.

## Can I cancel?

Free cancellation until 48 hours before departure. Inside 48 hours, there is no refund. A person at the office handles every cancellation.

## When does the cruise leave, and how long is it?

The cruise lasts 4 hours. It departs at 9:00 and at 14:00, May 15 to September 15.

## Is the cabin heated? Are there restrooms?

The cabin is heated and enclosed. There are two restrooms on board.

## Will we see whales?

Wildlife sightings change from trip to trip. We cannot promise any animal, including whales.

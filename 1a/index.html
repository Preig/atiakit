The pain it fixes

For the traveler

45% of US travelers name comparing multiple options as a frustrating moment in planning, the most-cited pain in the survey. When every listing says "unforgettable" and "family-friendly", there is nothing to compare.

For your business

More of the short list now comes from AI tools: 34% of US travelers use AI while researching a trip. In the report's test, one hotel question went to five AI tools and no hotel appeared in all five answers. Hotels with specific, documented amenities tended to come up more consistently than those described as "family friendly". Specific facts, published the same way everywhere, are the part you control.

Source: Skift Research + McKinsey, Winning Hearts in an Age of Infinite Travel Choices, Sept 2026 · survey of 1,015 US travelers, data as of June 2026 · US travelers, not Alaska visitors. The five-tool test is described in the same report. Figures last verified 2026-10-10.

Also fits: any listing on any channel. A lodge room page, a B&B listing, a fishing charter, a flightseeing tour, a DMO member page.

      How to use it

      1. Fill the fact sheet, or load the fictional example to see how it works. Tick Not supplied for anything you cannot confirm today. The prompt then says [FILL] instead of letting the AI guess.

      5. Paste each version into the channel checker. Fix anything missing or different.
      6. Fill every [FILL] from your records, publish all versions the same day, and fix your website to match Recommendation

      What this checker can and cannot do
      
            It looks for the numbers, times, dates, prices, ages and group sizes in each fact, plus its key words. It treats "9 am" and "9:00", or "48 hours" and "2 days", as the same. It can miss a fact that is reworded, and it can flag a harmless difference. Treat every flag as a question for a person, not a verdict.

            The one-listing test
            
    
            Monday tool · Pains 1 and 2 · about an hour
            
    
            Five yes-or-no checks: does your busiest page prove what your marketing promises? One point per Yes. Keep the score and rescore in 30 days.
            
    
            
      
            
        1. Does the page match the post?
        
            Same promise, season and photo style as what sent them here.
            
        
            
      
            
      
            
        2. Three proofs above the fold?
        
            A dated review, a real photo, a named person.
            
        
            
      
            
      
            
        3. One soft next step?
        
            "Check dates", "Ask a question" or "Save this trip", not only "Book now".
            
        
            
      
            
      
            
        4. Same facts everywhere?
        
            Price, hours, meeting point and terms match Google and your OTAs. The channel checker above helps.

            Saves, repeat visits or questions asked: count at least one.

              How you will know it worked
              
    
              Number 1 · AI visibility score: the Appear and Described checks
              
    
              Before you publish, run the AI visibility audit in three AI tools and write down two of its four checks for each answer. Appear: are you in the answer at all? Described: right category, place and promise? Each scores 0, 1 or 2, so five prompts give up to 20 per tool for these two checks. Rescore quarterly.
              
    
              Expect no change for weeks: AI tools recrawl on their own schedule, and one week's answers are noise. Report trends over a quarter, not a single answer Recommendation
              
    
              Number 2 · Direct share of bookings
              
    
              Bookings on your own site or by phone, divided by all bookings. Count it monthly, starting before you change anything, so you have a baseline.

              Before you use it
              
    
              AI drafts. You approve. Every prompt here asks the AI to mark its output DRAFT - PENDING OWNER REVIEW. A person who knows the operation checks every fact against your records before anything is published. Asking the AI to check its own work is not verification.
              
    
                
      
              • Legal mandate Claims in a listing may not be deceptive (FTC Act Section 5, 15 U.S.C. 45). A checkable fact has to be true on the day a traveler reads it. Accessibility, safety and "guaranteed" claims carry the most risk: describe what you have measured.
                • 
      
              • Legal mandate Lodging only: the price you show has to be the total price, including mandatory fees (FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees, 16 CFR Part 464, in effect since May 12, 2025). The rule covers short-term lodging and live-event tickets, not tours.
                • 
      
              • Legal mandate No fake reviews or testimonials, including AI-written ones (FTC Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials, 16 CFR Part 465). Quote real reviews only as written.
                • 
      
              • Legal mandate Guide licensing and registration are yours and cannot be delegated, to staff or to an AI tool (AS 08.54 for big game guides; ADF&G registration for sport fishing guides and businesses).
                • 
      
              • Contractual Before you change a price or terms on one channel, check your Viator supplier agreement and any other OTA, reseller or cruise line contract for rate parity and for how each platform displays cancellation terms.
                • 
      
              • Policy choice Your weather, cancellation and refund terms are your decisions. The AI words them; it does not set them.
                • 
      
              • Recommendation Safety-critical wording (weather go/no-go, sea state, glacier, wildlife) is drafted by AI and owned by your captain or operations lead. Do not promise wildlife, weather or views.
                • 
      
              • Recommendation No guest names, emails, phone numbers, card numbers, IDs or medical information in any box here or in any AI tool. Check the AI tool's data-use setting before you paste business material.
                • 
      
              • Recommendation Publish all versions the same day, keep the dated fact sheet, and recheck it every season. If a listing mentions a license or permit, show it exactly as issued.

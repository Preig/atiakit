45% Pain 1 Comparison overload

Traveler: too many options and no way to tell them apart. 63% would pick ten curated options over 500 with filters.

What it costs you (my read): not on the short list means not in the decision, and AI increasingly writes the short list.

  • Basic · 1.a Rewrite for the short list Generic adjectives out, checkable facts in, published the same on every channel. About 1 hour · free tier
  • Intermediate · 1.b Your own curated ten A "Which trip fits you?" page: three picks per traveler type, each with its reason. A week of evenings · paid tier, about $20 a month
  • Advanced · 1.c AI answer monitoring + structured data A weekly prompt panel across AI tools, plus schema.org markup so machines can read your facts. 2 to 4 weeks · developer or vendor · model API fees

43% Pain 2 Second-guessing

Traveler: the booking never feels final. 52% feel anxious about booking the wrong option.

What it costs you (my read): doubt shows up twice, as abandoned inquiries before the booking and as changes and cancellations after it.

  • Basic · 2.a The honest picture Reviews mined for what guests wish they knew, a "What to expect, honestly" section, and reply drafts. About 2 hours · free tier · a person posts replies
  • Intermediate · 2.b Grounded answer assistant A staff assistant that answers only from your facts, cites them, and otherwise says "Not in our facts. Ask the office." 1 day to set up, then a monthly check · paid tier
  • Advanced · 2.c Post-booking retention engine Exposure scoring, drafted offers in an approval queue, and a holdout group to test the gain. 4 to 8 weeks · developer plus booking-system API

39% Pain 3 Coordinating the trip

Traveler: nobody holds the whole trip together. Nine research channels are each used by roughly a third or more of travelers.

What it costs you (my read): an Alaska trip spans several businesses, and every handoff brings repeat questions and missed add-ons.

  • Basic · 3.a The AI trip kit A one-page plan per booking: partner options, what to wear, a weather plan B, and post-booking messages. 1 hour to build, then minutes per booking · free tier
  • Intermediate · 3.b Partner circle with shared facts One fact sheet shared by three to five partners, and a kit drafted automatically a month out. About a week · no-code automation, roughly $20 to $30 a month plus cents per kit
  • Advanced · 3.c Agent-ready trip record A structured trip record and reversible holds an AI agent can use. For most operators, it starts as one question to your booking platform. 15 minutes to ask · months to build, with a developer or vendor, or ATIA-wide

Your first 30 days

One sitting a week. Each week ends with something written down.

  1. Week 1: run the AI visibility audit and write down your score per AI tool. Then 1.a Rewrite for the short list.
  2. Week 2: 2.a The honest picture. Publish the "What to expect, honestly" section. A person edits and posts the review replies.
  3. Week 3: 3.a The AI trip kit and the post-booking messages (in 3.a). Decide what you do when a guest finds a lower price inside the cancellation window. Policy choice
  4. Week 4: count the four numbers below and write the baselines. Then choose one intermediate rung from the map for next quarter. Bring your scores to a no-cost Alaska SBDC advising session at aksbdc.org. Recommendation

5 tools

got the same family-hotel question from McKinsey: three AI portals and two OTA AI tools. No hotel appeared in all five answers; most appeared only once.

For the traveler

An AI tool hands the traveler a short list. The traveler compares what is on that list, not everything that exists.

For your business

If an AI tool leaves you off, or describes you wrong, you lose the comparison before it starts, and you never find out. This audit is how you find out. Check three tools, not one, because they disagree.

Source: Skift Research + McKinsey, Winning Hearts in an Age of Infinite Travel Choices, Sept 2026 · survey of n = 1,015 US travelers, data as of June 2026 (Exhibit 6; pages 22 and 24). US travelers, not Alaska visitors.

How to run it

  1. Fill in your details in Step 1. Public business names only.
  2. Pick three AI tools. Use whichever three are current. This kit does not recommend one. Free tiers work.
  3. Run each prompt in a fresh chat in each tool. Paste it exactly as written.
  4. Score each answer 0, 1 or 2 on four checks: Appear, Described, Cited, Current. Not in the answer at all? Score 0 on all four.
  5. Read your fix list. It starts with your weakest check.
  6. Save the result and re-run the same five prompts every quarter.

Answers vary by day and by tool. Ask the same question tomorrow and the answer may change. One run is a snapshot, not a ranking. Re-run quarterly, the same way each time, and compare your saved results. That is your Number 1: AI visibility score.

Step 2 · The five prompts

Run each prompt in a fresh chat, in three AI tools. Use whichever three are current; this kit does not recommend one.

  • Start a new chat for every prompt, so one answer does not shape the next.
  • If the tool has memory or personalization, use a temporary or private chat if it offers one. It may already know you own the business.
  • Paste the prompt exactly. Do not add your website and do not ask follow-up questions. You are scoring what a traveler would see.
  • Note the sources or links the tool shows. You need them for the Cited check.
  • The tool may use your location. Run it the same way each quarter so the results compare.

      Always
      
      
      Ask your booking platform about AI and metasearch feeds
      
      
      Google's Things to do and hotel price listings come from booking feeds sent by your booking platform or a connectivity partner, not from your website. Some AI assistants now show and book activities through OTAs and booking platforms too. One email finds out whether you are in those feeds.
      
      
      Question for your booking platform
      

      Hello. We use your platform for our bookings. Please answer in writing:
1. Do you send our live availability and prices to Google (Things to do, or hotel prices) or to other metasearch sites? Is it switched on for our account?
2. Do you connect to any AI assistants or AI booking programs? Which ones, and are we included?
3. What do we have to switch on, what does it cost, and is it covered by our current agreement?
4. If it is not available today, is it on your roadmap, and when?
      
      
      
      
      
      
      Recommendation Send the question and keep the written answer.
      
      
      Contractual What your platform shares, and what it costs, is set by your booking platform agreement. Check it before you switch anything on.
